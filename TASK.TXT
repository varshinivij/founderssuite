WHAT ARE WE BUILDING

–  End-to-end feedback flow that enables quality matches, community feed, insights, and summary reports.
–  Machine Learning driven recommendation algorithm: Matches founders with target users based on customer-discovery needs and users' lived experience, rather than demographics.
–  Core infrastructure
    –  Fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLM) for natural language processing
    –  Developing a Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) recommendation algorithm with domain-specific considerations
       –  Example: validating a biotech product requires testers with specialized knowledge (e.g., regulatory awareness), whereas a vehicle device company needs users with hands-on experience (e.g., usage context). The model must weight expertise and context differently rather than treating all users the same.
    –  Initial data pulled from public source repositories such as Kaggle
–  AI powered insights: Key findings extracted from validation interviews
    –  Nudges to founders on biased or leading questions
    –  Post-interview generated summary reports with actionable steps

KEY ADVANTAGE:
–  Current systems primarily rely on demographic filtering (such as problem domain and price range) rather than deeper contextual matching
–  Focus on relevant, experience-based users rather than broad, generic pools
–  Founder generates more data about their product with time, which enhances matching precision and scalability
–  Competitors cannot easily replicate the model because it depends on proprietary data, continuous feedback loops, and domain-specific model tuning that compound over time
